How We Use Your Information

We use collected information exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the MotionZync platform and all its features
  • To authenticate admin users via Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication)
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense, which helps keep MotionZync free
  • To analyze usage patterns anonymously via Google Analytics to improve the platform
  • To respond to your inquiries when you contact us via email or the contact form
  • To review and publish animations submitted through the submission form (after admin approval)
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or malicious code submissions
  • To comply with legal obligations including applicable laws and regulations
⚠️ We never sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for profiling, automated decision-making, or targeted marketing beyond AdSense's standard ad personalization.

Firebase & Google Authentication

MotionZync uses Google Firebase for backend services and authentication. Firebase is a product of Google LLC.

  • Firebase Firestore: Stores animation data (code, metadata, tags) in a secure cloud database. No personal user data is stored in Firestore unless you submit an animation.
  • Firebase Authentication: Used exclusively for administrator login via Google Sign-In. Regular visitors do not need an account and no auth data is stored for them.
  • Firebase Hosting / Functions: Used to serve the application securely.

Firebase handles authentication in compliance with Google's security standards. We do not have access to your Google password. Only your name, email, and profile picture are shared with us when an admin logs in.

Firebase's data processing is governed by Google Firebase Privacy & Security documentation.

Data Security

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your information:

  • Sandboxed iframes: All animation code runs inside sandboxed iframes, completely isolated from your device and our systems
  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and MotionZync is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Firebase Security Rules: Firestore data is protected by server-side security rules that restrict unauthorized access
  • No password storage: We never store passwords — authentication is fully handled by Google's secure infrastructure
  • Minimal data collection: We collect the minimum data necessary, reducing security risk by design
  • Regular security reviews: Our codebase is reviewed for security vulnerabilities
⚠️ Important: While we take reasonable precautions, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to MotionZync.

Children's Privacy

MotionZync is a general audience platform intended for users of all ages, including students learning web development. However, we take children's privacy very seriously.

  • MotionZync does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EU/EEA under GDPR)
  • Our platform does not require account creation for regular users — children can use all public features without providing any personal information
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will delete such information
  • Google AdSense is configured to serve age-appropriate content. We do not intentionally target advertising to children

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international children's privacy laws.

Data Retention

We retain different categories of data for different periods:

  • Analytics data: Retained for 26 months by Google Analytics (anonymized after 14 months)
  • Admin authentication data: Retained while the admin account remains active
  • Submitted animation code: Retained indefinitely as part of the platform content unless deletion is requested
  • Contact messages: Retained for up to 12 months for response purposes, then deleted
  • Ad cookies: Controlled by Google AdSense (typically up to 2 years)

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at wealthkavach1@gmail.com.
